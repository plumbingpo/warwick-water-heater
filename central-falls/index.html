Getting More Years From Your Water Heater in Central Falls

The best way to avoid a surprise leak is routine maintenance. We recommend that homeowners flush the tank once a year (more often with hard water), have the anode rod checked every three to five years, test the relief valve once a year to make sure it isn't stuck, and keep the thermostat around 120 degrees, which reduces the risk of scalding and slows mineral buildup. Tankless owners should plan on descaling every year in hard-water areas.

The age of your heater matters just as much. With regular maintenance, a standard tank typically lasts 8 to 12 years, a heat pump water heater 10 to 15, and a tankless unit up to 20. Once a heater passes that range and starts showing warning signs, like rust-colored water, popping noises, or a damp spot around the base, it's smart to start planning a replacement. A planned replacement costs less and causes far less stress than an emergency swap after a leak.

The earlier you report a hot water problem, the cheaper it usually is. A sediment layer that's just starting to rumble needs only a flush, yet that same buildup, neglected through a few more years, can overheat the bottom of the tank and shorten the life of the tank, turning a simple visit into a replacement. Save our number in your contacts now so you can reach us fast the moment something sounds or looks wrong. A quick call saves time, money, and ruined floors.

Common Water Heater Problems in Central Falls

In Central Falls, we work on every kind of water heater, and each one has a typical way of failing. Gas tank heaters tend to have trouble with pilot assemblies and thermocouples, sooty burners, and venting that doesn't draft. Electric units burn out elements and thermostats, particularly when sediment buries the lower element. Tankless units show error codes for scale in the heat exchanger, ignition failures, and flow sensor problems, most of which trace back to skipped descaling. Hybrid heat pump units depend on a clean filter and a free-flowing condensate line, and they don't do well in small, closed-in spaces. Our technicians work on all four every week and stock the common parts to finish most repairs in one trip.
