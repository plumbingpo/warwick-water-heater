Restaurant Services

Restaurant and Food Service Water Heaters

In a restaurant, the dish machine, the sinks, and the handwashing stations all pull from the same supply, and a heater that's undersized or failing reveals itself as lukewarm water at the dish machine and stacks of dirty plates. A high-temperature dish machine typically needs final rinse water near 180°F, which usually means a booster heater paired with the main supply. We handle high-input tanks, tankless racks, booster heaters, and sizing for peak demand, and we schedule every visit around your prep and service times.

Temperature is where many restaurant hot water problems start. When the main heater can't keep up, handwashing sinks go lukewarm, the three-compartment sink falls below temperature, and the dish machine struggles to sanitize during the rush. We verify temperatures at the tank, the booster, and the fixtures and document every visit in written reports your inspector and landlord can review.

We work directly with owners, managers, and kitchen staff to plan replacements and maintenance that protect your business and fit your operating schedule. Our technicians know how to work in occupied facilities, installing after close when needed, minimizing disruption, and keeping part of the system online where possible.

Code & Inspections

Installation, Inspection, and Record-Keeping

Safety is part of every commercial job we do. We inspect venting and draft, verify proper combustion air, test relief valves and their discharge lines, confirm expansion tanks are charged to the right pressure, and set mixing valves so water can be stored hot enough to control bacteria while reaching the tap at a safe temperature. Permits and inspections are handled as part of the job. Findings go into a written report with priority levels and specific recommended actions.

The paperwork matters as much as the repair. Every account gets a documentation binder with detailed service logs, temperature readings, and relief valve test results, so you can demonstrate due diligence to landlords, insurers, and inspectors. Heavy-use equipment, in kitchens, laundries, and central plant rooms, gets a more frequent visit schedule.

Emergency Service

After-Hours Commercial Water Heater Response

A hot water failure on a commercial property can shut down a kitchen, cancel a day of salon appointments, leave hotel guests with cold showers, and expose the owner to tenant disputes. Warwick Water Heater provides fast emergency water heater service for commercial properties across Warwick and surrounding areas, and we schedule non-urgent work after hours or before opening to limit downtime.

Failed gas valves, burned-out elements, ignition faults, tripped high-limit switches, leaking tanks, stalled recirculation pumps, tankless lockouts: our technicians handle all of it on commercial equipment. Our first priority is pinpointing the fault and restoring hot water as quickly as possible, often by isolating the failed heater so the others carry the load. Once it's running, we put maintenance in place to prevent a repeat, and each response comes with full documentation for your facility files.

Preventive Care

Proactive Water Heater Care for Businesses

Preventing failures is the most cost-effective way to manage commercial water heaters. Every Warwick Water Heater program is tailored to the property's equipment, usage, and budget, and includes scheduled inspections, sediment flushing, anode rod checks, descaling for tankless units, relief valve testing, burner and vent inspection, recirculation and mixing valve checks, and temperature readings at the fixtures.

Scheduled maintenance typically costs a fraction of an emergency replacement and significantly reduces the risk of downtime and tenant complaints. Properties on a program usually see fewer outages, longer equipment life, and steadier water temperatures. Call us to discuss a plan designed for your property's needs and budget.
